# 12kW Off-Grid Solar PV System Design & Simulation (PVsyst)

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the complete engineering design and simulation for a **12kW Stand-Alone (Off-Grid) Solar Photovoltaic System**. The system is designed to provide 100% energy independence for a residential property with a daily consumption of **38.8 kWh/day**, ensuring high reliability and continuous power supply without grid connectivity.

---

## 📊 System Architecture & Specifications

### 1. Load Profile Analysis
* **Daily Energy Requirement:** 38.8 kWh/day
* **Peak Power Demand:** 3.66 kW
* **Design Strategy:** Performed dynamic hourly load distribution to precisely balance daytime direct consumption and nighttime battery storage needs.

### 2. PV Array Design (Solar Panels)
* **Module Model:** Jinko Solar 620 Wp (Si-mono)
* **Total Number of Modules:** 18 Panels
* **Total Array Power:** 11.16 kWp
* **Electrical Configuration:** 2 parallel strings × 9 modules in series.
* **Optimization:** Designed to ensure the Open-Circuit Voltage ($V_{oc}$ at -10°C = 106V) and Maximum Power Voltage ($V_{mpp}$) stay strictly within the safe operating window of the charge controller.

### 3. Energy Storage System (Battery Bank)
* **Battery Technology:** Lithium-ion (LiFePO4) - Pylontech US5000B
* **Nominal Voltage:** 48 V
* **Total Capacity:** 911 Ah
* **Configuration:** 1 Module in Series, 9 Modules in Parallel (1S / 9P)
* **Usable Capacity:** ~30.6 kWh (at 80% Depth of Discharge - DOD) to safely cover nighttime consumption and maximize battery lifespan (6000 cycles).

### 4. Controller & Power Conversion
* **Inverter/Controller Capacity:** 12 kW (Universal Hybrid/Off-Grid Topology)
* **Max Charging Current:** 255.8 A (Adaptive MPPT regulation)
* **Efficiency:** Optimized to eliminate overvoltage faults and minimize system losses.
